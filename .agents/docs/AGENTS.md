## Governance Directives
- **Zero Scope Creep:** Strictly adhere to C-LACTS boundaries (no biometric ingestion, no online DTR submission, no digital signatures, no monetary payroll).
- **Match the Diagrams & Forms:** As soon as physical forms, sample ledger cards, ERD, and DFDs are provided by the user, immediately cross-validate and update schemas.
