Legal Authority & Physical Artifact Verification

Historical and statutory codification of leave accruals and decimal conversions in the Philippine Civil Service.

1. Physical Evidence at HRMU Basud:

Verified scans of the primary reference binder filed at the Human Resource Management Unit (HRMU), Municipal Hall, Basud, Camarines Norte, explicitly titled "⭐ STARLA ⭐".

  • Cover Page: Explicitly marked SPECIAL PRIVILEGE LEAVE (3 DAYS) and MANDATORY FORCE LEAVE (5 DAYS).
  • Page 13: Table II: Vacation and Sick Leave Credits Earned on a Daily Basis (1 to 30 days).
  • Page 14: Table III: Leave Credits Earned with LWOP (Omitted per HRMU Basud operational policy; staff exclusively utilizes Table II and Table IV).
  • Page 15: Table IV: Conversion of Working Hours/Minutes into Fractions of a Day (8-Hour Workday; 1 to 8 Hours and 1 to 60 Minutes).

2. Statutory Civil Service Issuances:

  • Civil Service Commission Omnibus Rules on Leave (Rule XVI of Executive Order No. 292): Statutory framework governing leave accruals, mandatory forced leave, special privilege leave, and tardiness deductions.
  • CSC Memorandum Circular No. 41, s. 1998: Guidelines on the application and deduction of leave credits.
  • CSC Memorandum Circular No. 14, s. 1999: Prescribing the fractional conversion matrix for hours and minutes.
  • CSC Memorandum Circular No. 01, s. 2026: Institutionalization of Wellness Leave (5 working days).

3. Non-Negotiable System Guardrails (C-LACTS):

  • Guardrail #1: Zero raw biometric ingestion. System accepts only pre-verified aggregate summaries.
  • Guardrail #6: Double-deduction shield: automatically prevents deducting absences covered by approved leave applications.
  • Guardrail #7: Immutability of Starla conversion tables: multipliers are never hardcoded in controllers; all conversions strictly query starla_reference.
